# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who need to inspect, understand, and act on Substrate governance: voters, delegates, treasury participants, and operators.

## Product Purpose

SubGov is a multi-chain governance ledger that brings referenda, treasury activity, network operations, and community discussion into one place. Success means a visitor can see what needs attention, understand the context, and reach the right action quickly.

## Positioning

It joins governance decisions, treasury flow, and community context in one operational view instead of scattering them across protocol-specific pages.

## Operating Context

Users scan the product repeatedly during active decision windows, often switching chains and moving between high-level status, detail pages, and wallet actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Next.js app contains working routes for current governance, treasury, network, people, forum, and legacy governance surfaces. Existing routes and mock data remain available; older and archived surfaces should be discoverable without competing with current work.

## Product Principles

- Surface what is active before what is historical.
- Keep the path from signal to detail to action short.
- Group protocol complexity behind clear operational categories.
- Preserve access to legacy records without making them primary navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, and responsive layouts are required for the web interface.
